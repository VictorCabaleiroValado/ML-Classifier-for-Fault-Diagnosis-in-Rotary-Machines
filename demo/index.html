What this demo proves

One example per category is chosen by its lowest held-out row index, without selecting for successful predictions.

Academic demonstration, not a validated maintenance tool. Labels come from source filenames. Measurements from the same acquisition may be related, so this split does not establish performance on independent machines or runs. No calibrated confidence score is claimed.

Source measurement and reproducibility

Time-domain decision tree · seed 42 · 80/20 split stratified by category. Training excludes every demo example. Signal summaries are verified against the reconstructed feature table before export.
